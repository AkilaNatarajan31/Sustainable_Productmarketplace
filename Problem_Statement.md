Problem Statement

1. Title

Sustainable Product Marketplace

2. Domain

E-Commerce / Sustainability

3. Who is the User?

1. Customer

Customers can register, log in, browse sustainable products, search and filter products, add products to their cart, place orders, and provide reviews.

2. Seller

Sellers can register, manage their seller profile, add and manage sustainable products, maintain stock, and manage customer orders.

3. Admin

The Admin manages users, verifies sellers, manages products and categories, and monitors marketplace activities.

4. What Problem Are We Solving?

Many customers want to buy eco-friendly products but find it difficult to identify and compare sustainable products on regular e-commerce platforms. Small businesses that produce sustainable products also face difficulties in reaching customers and getting visibility online. For example, a customer looking for a reusable or biodegradable product may have to search through many unrelated products on a general marketplace. The proposed system provides a dedicated marketplace where customers can easily discover sustainable products from sellers.

5. Proposed Solution

The Sustainable Product Marketplace is a web-based e-commerce application dedicated to sustainable and eco-friendly products.

* The application will provide:
* Customer registration and login
* Seller registration and management
* Admin seller approval
* Sustainable product listing
* Product categories
* Product search and filtering
* Product details
* Shopping cart
* Wishlist
* Checkout and order placement
* Order history and status
* Product ratings and reviews
* Seller product and inventory management
* Admin dashboard
* User, seller, product, and category management

6. Core Entities / Database Tables

The main database tables are:

1.Users
2.Sellers
3.Categories
4.Products
5.Cart
6.Cart_Items
7.Orders
8.Order_Items
9.Reviews
10.Wishlist
11.Payments

7. User Roles & Permissions

Admin

* Manage users
* Approve or reject sellers
* Manage categories
* Manage products
* Monitor orders and marketplace activities

Seller

* Manage seller profile
* Add, edit, and delete products
* Manage product stock
* View and manage orders
* Customer
* Register and log in
* Browse, search, and filter products
* Add products to cart and wishlist
* Place orders
* View order history
* Submit product reviews

8. Success Criteria

* A customer should be able to register and log in successfully.
* A customer should be able to find a required sustainable product using search and filters.
* A customer should be able to add a product to the cart and place an order successfully.
* A seller should be able to add and manage products successfully.
* An admin should be able to approve sellers and manage marketplace data.
* The completed application should be accessible through a public URL during Review II.

9. Out of Scope

The following features will not be included in the initial project:
* Native Android/iOS mobile application
* International shipping
* Multi-language support
* Real-time chat between customers and sellers
* Advanced logistics/shipping integration
* Real-time delivery tracking
* International payment systems
* Complex AI features in the core version
These features can be considered as future enhancements if time permits.

10. Chosen Track
